1 · The loop

Drivesleep, eat, clean, move, belong, create
→
Motives7 meters decay in time: energy, hunger, fun, focus, hygiene, bladder, social
→
Desireutility + noise + emotion pull + clock window
→
Chaina sequence of acts; breaks branch to the next sequence
→
Act45 acts, each with a motive reward / drain
→
Outcomeok · partial · fail · side · surprise
→
Ledgerevents add/deduct points, leak across motives
→
ChemistryDA HT NE CORT OXY SLP
→
Emotion27 states with hysteresis

Side inputs: hazards (erratic events: knock, phone ring, …) interrupt chains; a visitor is a social reward; the clock gates windows; MIDI and art are acts that pay rewards.
